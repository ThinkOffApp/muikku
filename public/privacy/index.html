Muikku Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 22, 2026

🛡️ Core Principle

Muikku is a local-first audio visualizer. Your privacy is our priority. Audio processing occurs entirely on your device.

What We Collect

  • Audio Data (Temporary): Microphone input is processed locally in real-time. It is never transmitted, recorded, or saved to any cloud server.
  • Usage Telemetry: Basic, anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the app.
  • Account Information (If you sign in): Basic profile information via Google or Firebase Authentication (used for linking purchases).
  • Purchase History: Standard Google Play billing information for in-app purchases.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your Audio/Voice: Never sent to our servers.
  • ❌ Local Files: Audio files loaded into the app are processed locally and never uploaded.
  • ❌ Payment Information: We do not store credit card details (handled securely by Google Play).

🔒 Data Security

  • Audio visualization uses local Digital Signal Processing (DSP) algorithms. No audio leaves your device.
  • Authentication and purchase state are handled via secure, industry-standard protocols (Firebase, Google Play).

👤 Your Rights

  • You can clear the app's cache and data at any time from your device settings.
  • You can revoke the Microphone permission at any time in your device settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account by contacting support.
